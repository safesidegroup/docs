Working with SafeSide is guided by collaboration, care, and clarity. Whatever your organization's size, setting, or starting point, you won't be handed a generic training package and left to figure it out alone. SafeSide works alongside you to understand your goals, map a realistic pathway, and stay involved throughout implementation — because lasting prevention requires more than a one-time event.

## Your Step-by-Step Journey With SafeSide

Every engagement begins with a conversation and progresses through a structured, proven process that keeps your needs at the center.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Schedule a Call">
    The first step is a straightforward conversation. SafeSide's team takes time to understand your organization's goals, your current context, and what success looks like for you. There's no sales pressure — this is about making sure you and SafeSide are the right fit and that the right programs are on the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discover and Plan">
    Together, you'll explore which SafeSide programs best match your needs. The team considers your available resources, budget constraints, and intended outcomes. You'll get honest guidance on what's realistic and what will deliver the most meaningful impact for your workforce and the people you serve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Program Pathway">
    Using a proven implementation model — Exploration, Preparation, Implementation, and Sustainment — SafeSide maps out a phased, tailored pathway for your organization. This isn't a template dropped into your inbox; it's a plan built around your specific situation, stakeholder landscape, and timelines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Getting Started">
    Once you're ready to move forward, SafeSide puts things in motion: access agreements are finalized, materials are prepared, and any orientation or onboarding for your team is arranged. You'll begin with a clear picture of who does what and when.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens After You Decide to Move Forward

Committing to a SafeSide program is the beginning of a structured, supported implementation — not a hand-off. Here's what you can expect once you're ready to proceed:

1. **Finalize the plan** — SafeSide confirms timelines, contracts, and roles so every stakeholder knows what to expect and when.
2. **Training and onboarding** — Where needed, your team leads and hosts receive orientation to ensure they're equipped to deliver or support the program effectively.
3. **Work begins** — Program content, support tools, and resources are delivered according to your agreed pathway. Delivery can be phased across departments, sites, or cohorts to suit your rollout needs.
4. **Monitoring and feedback loops** — SafeSide stays involved after launch, tracking progress and adjusting the approach based on evaluation data and your feedback.

<Note>
  SafeSide doesn't walk away after your program launches. Ongoing support, monitoring, and access to continually updated materials are built into the partnership — because sustaining change is as important as starting it.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does the process take?">
    The timeline depends on the scope of your engagement. For simpler, single-site setups, things can move quickly — sometimes within weeks. For more customized or organization-wide pathways involving multiple sites, programs, or stakeholder groups, SafeSide works with you to set clear milestones upfront. You'll always know what to expect and when.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are the costs?">
    Costs vary based on the program you choose and the size of your organization. SafeSide aims to keep pricing transparent and aligned with your goals and budget. During your discovery call, the team will walk you through the options that fit your situation — there are no surprises. Grant funding support and flexible partnership structures are also available for qualifying organizations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to customize SafeSide's programs to use them?">
    No. SafeSide's programs are designed to be used as-is across diverse workforces, settings, and populations. They are built to unite and engage teams working in varied roles, geographies, and cultural contexts under a common framework of best practices.

    That said, for organizations that want a more tailored learning experience — aligned to a specific population, location, or cultural context — SafeSide's **InPlace Productions** team partners with you to co-fund and co-develop customized content. This ranges from light adaptation of existing modules all the way to purpose-built programs crafted in collaboration with your team, local lived experience advisors, and community stakeholders.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  If you're unsure which program is the right starting point, the discovery call is the best place to begin. SafeSide's team will help you think through your priorities, constraints, and readiness — and recommend a pathway that fits where you are right now, not where you think you should be.
</Tip>
