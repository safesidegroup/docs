SafeSide unites diverse workforces around a common language and shared best practices for suicide prevention — and the programs work exactly as delivered for most organizations. But when your setting, population, or culture calls for something more tailored, SafeSide's development team partners with you to build a learning experience that truly looks and feels like yours.  

<Note>
  Customization is entirely optional. SafeSide's core programs are designed from the ground up to engage and support diverse workforces across a wide range of sectors, settings, and communities. If your workforce serves a broad or varied population, the standard content is almost certainly the right starting point — and for many organizations, it is all they ever need.
</Note>

## How Customization Works

SafeSide's approach to customization is iterative, collaborative, and feedback-driven. You are involved at every stage, from initial scoping through final delivery.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Explore">
    The SafeSide team listens to your needs and gathers input from your key stakeholders — leaders, clinical staff, lived experience contributors, and subject matter experts. This stage clarifies your learning objectives, audience, and any cultural or sector-specific requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare">
    SafeSide's instructional design team collaboratively develops materials through structured feedback sessions. This includes scripting, storyboarding, and incorporating diverse perspectives to ensure the content is both accurate and relevant to your context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implement and Sustain">
    Finalized materials are delivered and integrated into your program. Optional training resources and implementation guidance are available to support your team in rolling out the customized content across your workforce.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The timeline for customization depends on the scope of work. Clear milestones are agreed upon at the outset so you always know what to expect.

## Customization Levels

Collaboration with clinical staff, lived experience contributors, and subject matter experts is central to every customization SafeSide undertakes. The table below outlines the three levels available — choose the one that best matches your workforce's needs.

| Level | Best For | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Level 1: Add-ons** | Organizations using standard content with targeted enhancements | Leadership introductions, lived experience perspectives, documentation workflows, and practice prompts |
| **Level 2: Adaptation** | Organizations wanting core teachings contextualized for their sector | Sector-specific scenarios, role-based guidance, and implementation frameworks |
| **Level 3: Comprehensive** | Organizations reviewing their entire suicide prevention approach | Cultural assessment, policy review, pathway redesign, practice guidelines, and custom education |

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is customization required to use SafeSide's programs?">
    No. SafeSide's programs are used by organizations as-is and are designed to engage, unite, and support diverse workforces serving diverse populations across diverse locations — all thinking, acting, and communicating with common principles and best practices.

    For organizations that need a more tailored learning experience, SafeSide Prevention's instructional design and video production team — called the InPlace Productions team — partners with collaborating entities to fund and tailor content for maximum relevance and learning transfer in your specific setting, population, and culture.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does the customization production process involve?">
    All customization engagements include instructional design, scripting, storyboarding, pre- and post-production, and integration into program materials and workbooks.

    The process does require your active participation: identifying and connecting with local individuals who will appear in the content, providing learning objectives (particularly around local practice requirements), and collaborating on storyboards that reflect your organizational context and needs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is customization priced?">
    There is no fixed price for customization — the cost is determined by the scope of production required, and SafeSide works with you to right-size that to your budget and goals.

    Customization is made affordable through two approaches. First, SafeSide draws on in-house resources and tested production processes for almost all production needs. Second, a cost-sharing model is used: SafeSide co-funds the production with the contractual understanding that the resulting materials may be used to serve other organizations and advance the broader suicide prevention mission — with the collaborating organization's contributions always acknowledged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who produces the customized content?">
    All customized content is produced by the InPlace Productions team — SafeSide Prevention's in-house instructional design and video production unit. This team has developed and refined a repeatable, high-quality production process, meaning you benefit from professional-grade output without the cost of an external production agency.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
