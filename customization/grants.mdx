## Garrett Lee Smith / SAMHSA Grant

The Garrett Lee Smith Memorial Act funds suicide prevention efforts across health, behavioral health, and community-serving organizations in the United States. SafeSide CARE is well suited to GLS-funded programs, offering an evidence-informed education model that aligns with Zero Suicide principles and supports sustainable implementation over the grant period.

SafeSide has prepared application-ready guidance and sample language specifically for the Garrett Lee Smith grant. [Access the GLS application support page](https://info.safesideprevention.com/samhsa-garrett-lee-smith-grant) to get started.

### SafeSide CARE Adaptations for Grant Applications

SafeSide CARE is available in four sector-specific adaptations. When writing your application, select the adaptation that best matches your organizational context:

| Adaptation | Designed For |
|---|---|
| **Mental Health** | Inpatient and outpatient clinical services; psychiatrists, psychologists, counselors, social workers, and non-clinical colleagues including peer workers |
| **Primary Health** | General health care practices; doctors, nurses, specialists, and their administrative support teams |
| **Youth Services** | Services working with children, youth, and young adults up to age 25; clinical and non-clinical roles |
| **Alcohol and Other Drugs** | Substance use treatment services; counselors, allied health, social workers, and peer workers |

SafeSide CARE does not replace existing evidence-based tools — it provides a unifying framework within which existing instruments such as C-SSRS, ASQ, SAFE-T, and Brief Safety Planning Intervention can be integrated.

## Implementation Phases Aligned with Grant Requirements

Grant funders typically expect a structured implementation plan. SafeSide CARE is delivered in three phases that map directly to standard grant deliverables:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Exploration">
    Organizations complete a one-time configuration and setup process supported by the SafeSide team. This phase covers program orientation, account configuration, and readiness planning — typically completable in 3–5 days, so you can launch quickly once funding is secured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preparation">
    Staff complete video-guided workshops in small groups, led by on-screen clinical and lived experience experts with structured discussion prompts. No internal trainer or facilitation expertise is required, making this phase scalable across distributed workforces and multiple sites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implementation and Sustainment">
    Ongoing engagement is maintained through monthly Questions & Updates sessions with SafeSide faculty, weekly microlearnings, refresher modules, and access to a global community of practice. This sustained model ensures training is not a one-time event but a continuous workforce development program — supporting new staff onboarding and skill development throughout the full grant period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## InPlace Learning Alignment with Grant Deliverables

SafeSide CARE is delivered through InPlace® Learning, a scalable model that supports the workforce development outcomes funders look for. Key features that strengthen grant applications include:

- **No train-the-trainer dependency** — organizations can launch and sustain the program without relying on a small number of internal trainers
- **Real-time evaluation dashboard** — provides data on participation, completion, and learning outcomes to support funder reporting requirements
- **Ongoing live learning** — monthly faculty sessions and In Focus deep dives demonstrate continuous engagement beyond initial training
- **Community of Practice** — staff connect with peers across organizations, supporting knowledge sharing and sustainability
- **Evidence base** — InPlace® Learning builds on more than a decade of research, including published efficacy studies among mental health professionals and youth services workers

## Sample Grant Language

The following text can be adapted for use directly in your application:

> **\[Organization Name\]** will partner with SafeSide Prevention to deliver workforce education aligned with the Zero Suicide framework, supporting **\[Organization Name\]** to implement suicide prevention practices across roles and settings. Education will focus on building a shared framework, strengthening workforce confidence and consistency, and supporting ongoing learning rather than one-time training. SafeSide Prevention will work alongside **\[Organization Name\]** to support leadership engagement, integration of lived experience, and continuous quality improvement.
