Most suicide prevention efforts focus on the individual clinician or a single point of intervention. SafeSide takes a fundamentally different approach — building the culture, practices, and workforce development that allow your whole agency to respond consistently, at every level, across every setting. Whether you're responsible for youth services, community health, justice, or social care, SafeSide provides a systems-level framework that scales from a single team to thousands of distributed staff across an entire department.

## Four Programs. One System.

Start with one program and expand from there. Each SafeSide offering addresses a different layer of organizational readiness — so your agency builds capability, confidence, and culture in the right sequence.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Core Connections" icon="people-group">
    Start here to strengthen the foundation. Core Connections builds team cohesion, reduces isolation, and supports staff wellbeing — creating the trust and resilience that makes every other program more effective. Suitable for **everyone in your agency**, regardless of role or setting.

    Delivered across **4–6 hours** of interactive facilitated sessions, this upstream prevention program uses the Four Core Strengths to build shared expectations of healthy coping and mutual support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SafeSide CARE" icon="heart-pulse">
    SafeSide CARE gives anyone who may encounter a person at risk — clinicians, receptionists, case managers, support staff — a **shared framework** for recognizing and responding to suicide risk. It builds consistency and confidence across every role that touches the people your agency serves.

    Delivered through **half-day InPlace® workshops** with ongoing microlearning, live Q&A sessions, and a community of practice so skills continue to develop long after the initial training.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CAMS Treatment" icon="stethoscope">
    For licensed clinicians only. CAMS (Collaborative Assessment and Management of Suicidality) is a structured, evidence-based treatment for suicidal ideation that engages patients as active partners in their own care. Backed by **35 years of research**, CAMS reduces suicidal ideation in as few as **6–8 sessions** with a trained therapist.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SafeSide Restore" icon="arrows-rotate">
    For clinical and quality teams. SafeSide Restore provides leaders with a **network for collaborating** with peer organizations to build a more restorative, just, and learning culture. Through bi-monthly working meetings and curated resources, your leaders gain the tools to shift from blame-focused incident review to processes centered on healing, learning, and systemic improvement.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Government-Scale Implementation

SafeSide has a demonstrated track record of large-scale deployment in government contexts, including partnerships with state agencies, youth justice systems, and health departments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assessment and Planning">
    SafeSide consultants work with your leadership team to map your agency's current capability, identify priority cohorts, and design an implementation plan that fits your operational structure and workforce distribution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pilot with Priority Teams">
    Many government agencies begin with CARE and CAMS in a priority division or cohort. This establishes a shared framework and language before rolling out more broadly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand Across Settings">
    Training is scaled to reach distributed and diverse staff — including those in community-based, residential, justice, and administrative roles — using the InPlace® Learning model, which requires no specialist facilitation expertise at the local level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sustain and Measure">
    Built-in evaluation tools track confidence, skills use, and cultural change over time. Core Connections' network effects and CARE's ongoing microlearning ensure capability is maintained rather than lost after an initial training day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does SafeSide support implementation across distributed settings?">
    SafeSide's InPlace® Learning model is specifically designed for organizations with distributed or geographically spread workforces. Because the instructional content is embedded in video, any nominated staff member can host a CARE workshop without specialist facilitation training — making it straightforward to deliver consistently across offices, community sites, and remote locations.

    For Core Connections, SafeSide provides expert facilitators in the early stages, with a pathway to prepare in-house trainers who can sustain the program at scale.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the time commitment for staff?">
    - **Core Connections:** 4–6 hours of interactive team sessions
    - **SafeSide CARE:** Half-day InPlace® workshop with ongoing microlearning
    - **CAMS Treatment:** 10 hours of online coursework plus 4 consultation calls (licensed clinicians only)
    - **Restore:** Bi-monthly network meetings for leaders

    All programs are designed to integrate into existing professional development schedules without requiring staff to step away for multi-day off-site events.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is training delivered?">
    Core Connections is delivered in interactive group sessions led by expert facilitators. SafeSide CARE is delivered through InPlace® Learning — a video-led workshop model that any nominated staff member can host — followed by ongoing live Q&A sessions, a community of practice, and weekly microlearnings. Both approaches can be scheduled flexibly to fit agency operations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can we start small and scale?">
    Yes. Many government agencies begin with CARE in a priority cohort — for example, youth services or case management teams — then expand to other divisions once a consistent framework is established. Core Connections can be added to build team wellbeing across the workforce, and Restore supports leadership culture change over time.

    SafeSide works alongside your agency at every stage, from initial planning through long-term implementation and evaluation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
