## How SafeSide CARE Works for Educators

SafeSide CARE uses a tiered approach so every staff member receives training that matches their role and level of contact with students — without overwhelming non-clinical staff or undersupporting mental health professionals.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Universal Tier — All Staff (30 minutes)">
    Every adult in your building completes a 30-minute eCourse that builds awareness and introduces the shared language of the SafeSide Framework. This ensures that even staff with minimal direct student contact — such as administrative or facilities staff — understand the basics and know who to contact if they have concerns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Targeted Tier — Teachers, Aides, Coaches, Admins (2 hours)">
    Staff who have regular direct contact with students complete a 2-hour workshop that builds confidence in recognizing distress, initiating caring conversations, and making appropriate referrals. The focus is on practical, role-specific skills — not clinical assessment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Intensive Tier — Mental Health Staff (2 hours)">
    School counselors, psychologists, and social workers complete an intensive 2-hour workshop that deepens their skills in structured risk assessment, person-centered care conversations, documentation, and safety planning aligned with the SafeSide Framework.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ongoing Learning">
    Following the initial workshop, all staff have access to ongoing microlearnings delivered weekly, live Q&A sessions with SafeSide experts, and a community of practice — so skills are reinforced over time rather than fading after a one-off training day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Real-World Scenario: Supporting a Student in Crisis

To illustrate how SafeSide CARE works in practice, consider the following scenario drawn from SafeSide's clinical demonstrations:

Liz, a school liaison, is working with **Selena**, a 15-year-old student who has disclosed suicidal thoughts after intimate photos were shared at school without her consent. Using the SafeSide Framework, Liz:

- **Connects** with Selena in a way that establishes trust and opens a genuine conversation about what she's experiencing
- **Assesses** the nature and context of Selena's distress in a structured but person-centered way — understanding her experience, not just checking boxes
- **Responds** by collaboratively developing a safety plan that reflects Selena's own strengths and support network
- **Extends** care by coordinating with Selena's family and other staff to ensure consistent support beyond the immediate conversation

This scenario reflects the kind of real, complex situation that educators face — and the SafeSide Framework gives staff the tools and confidence to navigate it with care.

## How SafeSide Differs from Existing Crisis Protocols

<Note>
  SafeSide does **not** replace your school's existing crisis response protocols. It strengthens them by adding a structured, preventive framework that helps staff recognize and respond to distress **earlier** — before a crisis escalates.
</Note>

Most schools already have crisis response plans in place for when a student is in acute danger. What many lack is a consistent approach to the earlier, harder-to-read moments — when a student is struggling but not yet in crisis. SafeSide fills this gap by giving every adult a clear role in the continuum of care, from the first sign of distress through to referral and follow-up. The result is that your existing crisis protocols are activated less often, and when they are, the people involved are better prepared.
