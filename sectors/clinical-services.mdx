Clinical services face a dual challenge: caring for people at their most vulnerable while simultaneously supporting staff who carry that responsibility every day. SafeSide partners with health and human services organizations to build confident care, connected teams, and a shared approach to suicide prevention — before, during, and after crisis. Whether you're strengthening an individual team or transforming practice across an entire system, SafeSide provides the programs, evidence, and ongoing partnership to make it work.

## Programs for Clinical Services

SafeSide brings together four connected programs designed to cover every dimension of suicide prevention in clinical settings — from staff wellbeing and daily practice to structured treatment and restorative learning after critical incidents.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Core Connections" icon="people-group">
    An interactive program that strengthens team connections and resilience, reduces isolation, and supports staff wellbeing. Delivered across **4–6 hours** of facilitated team sessions, Core Connections uses the Four Core Strengths — Kinship, Purpose, Guidance, and Balance — to build a shared language for healthy coping and mutual support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SafeSide CARE" icon="heart-pulse">
    CARE gives clinicians and non-clinical staff a shared framework for person-centered suicide-specific care — improving consistency, confidence, and collaboration across teams and roles. Delivered through **half-day InPlace® workshops** with ongoing microlearning and live Q&A sessions built in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CAMS Treatment" icon="stethoscope">
    The Collaborative Assessment and Management of Suicidality (CAMS) is an evidence-based, suicide-specific treatment that engages patients as active partners in their own care. Structured CAMS training — including a foundational video course, role-play practice day, and **4 consultation calls** — is proven to reduce suicidal ideation in as few as **6–8 sessions**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SafeSide Restore" icon="arrows-rotate">
    A bi-monthly network of leaders working to build restorative, just, and learning cultures in their organizations. Restore provides educational resources, live working sessions, and peer collaboration to help services respond to suicide-related incidents in ways that support healing, staff retention, and systemic improvement.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Starting Small and Scaling

You don't need to implement everything at once. Many clinical services begin with **SafeSide CARE and CAMS** in their priority teams to build shared language and structured treatment capability first. From there, they add **Core Connections** to strengthen team wellbeing and culture, then bring in **Restore** to address how their organization responds after critical incidents.

<Tip>
  Starting with CARE and CAMS allows your team to establish a consistent framework and build confidence quickly — before expanding to the broader cultural and wellbeing programs. SafeSide provides guidance at every stage of this journey.
</Tip>
