SafeSide's program add-ons are topic-specific, self-paced learning experiences designed to complement the SafeSide CARE program and deepen your team's skills in key areas of suicide prevention practice. Each module takes between 30 and 60 minutes to complete and can be undertaken at any point in your learning journey — before the SafeSide CARE workshop as preparation, afterward as a targeted deep-dive, or as a standalone resource for practitioners who want to strengthen a specific area of their work. All four modules are grounded in current evidence, include practical tools, and award a downloadable completion certificate upon finishing your post-evaluation.

## The Four Add-On Modules

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Developing Collaborative, Evidence-Based Plans for Safety" icon="clipboard-check">
    Learn the principles and practice of collaborative safety planning — one of the most evidence-supported interventions in suicide prevention. This module guides you through how to co-develop safety plans that are genuinely meaningful to the person at risk, including steps, supports, and means restriction strategies.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Asking Directly About Suicide Using the C-SSRS" icon="comments">
    Build confidence in using the Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) to ask direct, empathetic, and effective questions about suicide risk. This module provides practical tools and guidance for using this validated screening instrument in clinical and non-clinical settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Using Prevention-Oriented Risk Formulation for Suicide Concerns" icon="magnifying-glass-chart">
    Move beyond checklist-style risk assessment toward a prevention-oriented risk formulation approach. This module teaches you how to synthesize information about a person's situation, strengths, and vulnerabilities to guide meaningful clinical decision-making and care planning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enhancing Means Safety" icon="lock">
    Understand the evidence base for means safety counseling and develop the practical skills to have collaborative conversations about reducing access to lethal means. This module covers how to approach means safety in a way that is respectful, effective, and integrated into your broader care approach.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## When to Use the Add-Ons

<Steps>
  <Step title="Before SafeSide CARE (Preparation)">
    Use the add-on modules as structured preparation before your team's SafeSide CARE workshop. Completing a relevant module in advance helps participants arrive with foundational knowledge and greater readiness to engage with the group learning experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="After SafeSide CARE (Reinforcement)">
    Return to a specific add-on module after your SafeSide CARE workshop to deepen your skills in a targeted area. This is especially useful when you encounter a particular challenge in practice — such as safety planning complexity or means safety conversations — and want structured, evidence-based support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="As Standalone Learning">
    Use any add-on module independently of the SafeSide CARE program if there is a specific skill area your team or individual practitioners want to develop outside of a formal workforce education program.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Add-on modules are not included in a standard SafeSide CARE subscription. They are optional modules available to add onto your subscription. Contact the SafeSide team for a quote.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will I receive a completion certificate?">
    Yes. When you submit your post-evaluation at the end of each module, you will receive a downloadable certificate of completion. This can be used for continuing professional development records or workplace training documentation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is this included in my SafeSide subscription?">
    The add-on modules are not included as part of a standard SafeSide CARE subscription. They are optional modules available to SafeSide subscribers as an add-on. Reach out to the SafeSide team to discuss pricing and add them to your subscription.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long do the modules take to complete?">
    Each module is designed to be completed in 30 to 60 minutes. The self-paced format means you can pause and return as your schedule allows, and some learners prefer to take additional time to reflect on the content or revisit key sections before completing their post-evaluation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to complete these modules before or after SafeSide CARE?">
    These modules are designed to be completed at any point in your learning journey. They offer structured, evidence-based content that can serve as preparation before SafeSide CARE workshops, as a deeper dive afterward, or as completely standalone learning. There is no required sequence — choose the point that works best for your individual or team context.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
