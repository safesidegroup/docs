Core Connections is an upstream suicide prevention program designed to build cohesive, healthy teams in military, police, and first responder settings. Rather than waiting for crises to occur and responding to individuals who identify themselves as at-risk, Core Connections takes a proactive approach — strengthening the social bonds, shared expectations, and protective strengths of the whole group. Tested in a gold-standard randomized controlled trial (Wyman et al. 2020, 2022) with military personnel and known as Wingman Guardian Connect in the United States Air Force, Core Connections is proven to reduce depression, suicide risk, and work-related problems. Evaluations are currently underway in healthcare, religious communities, and youth sports settings.

## Why Teams Should Participate in Core Connections

Core Connections' network-based approach creates multiple layers of passive and active protection for at-risk individuals working in high-pressure environments — without requiring them to self-identify or their leaders to detect and intervene individually.

## The Four Core Strengths

Core Connections equips every participant with a shared framework of four protective strengths that build resilience, normalize help-seeking, and hold teams together under pressure.

<CardGroup cols={2}>

  <Card title="Purpose" icon="bullseye">

## Research and Evidence Base

Core Connections was developed by Dr. Peter Wyman and colleagues (2020, 2023) at the University of Rochester. A pivotal study published in **JAMA Network Open** demonstrated significant efficacy in reducing psychiatric diagnoses and medically documented suicidal behavior among new members of the US Air Force through the Wingman Guardian Connect program.

<Note>
  Core Connections is currently available for education, health, military, and first responder organizations. Adaptations are under evaluation in healthcare, religious communities, youth settings, and other high-pressure workplaces.
</Note>
