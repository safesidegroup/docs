SafeSide CARE is a suicide prevention workforce education program that gives your entire team — clinicians and non-clinical staff alike — a shared language, structured approach, and the confidence to act when someone is at risk. Delivered through the InPlace® Learning model, SafeSide CARE combines video-based teaching and real-world demonstrations with group interaction, followed by ongoing support to embed skills in everyday practice. Whether your team works in mental health, primary care, youth services, or alcohol and other drugs services, SafeSide CARE brings consistent, person-centred suicide prevention principles to everyone in the room.

## The SafeSide Framework (CARE)

SafeSide CARE is built around the SafeSide Framework — a map of evidence-based and best practices in suicide prevention. It goes beyond steps to build a common language and consistent practices for your people, no matter their role.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Connect" icon="comments">
    Develop skills to connect with people, ask directly about suicide, understand the person's lived experience, and establish a shared goal to help them feel better.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Assess" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Learn how to use a detailed model for synthesizing information, identifying resources, and planning person-centred care to understand and address suicide concerns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Respond" icon="clipboard-check">
    Structure your approach to planning that includes evidence-based interventions, referrals, safety plans, and family support plans to respond effectively to suicide concerns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Extend" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Enhance the impact of intervention through caring contacts, collaborative planning, warm handovers, and consistent care approaches that extend support beyond the treatment setting and into the community.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How SafeSide CARE Is Delivered

<Steps>
  <Step title="Half-Day InPlace® Workshop">
    Your team learns together through a structured, video-led InPlace® workshop featuring clinical teaching and lived experience perspectives. Real-world demonstrations, guided discussion, and practical application of the SafeSide Framework are at the core of the session. The workshop can be completed within a half day, with flexible delivery options to suit your service context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monthly Q&A Sessions">
    Subscribers gain access to monthly live Q&A sessions with SafeSide clinical and lived experience experts, providing ongoing support for applying the framework in practice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Community of Practice">
    An online Community of Practice connects your team with other SafeSide users worldwide — a space to ask questions, access tools and resources, and share experiences with a growing global network of practitioners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Weekly Microlearnings">
    Short, focused microlearning resources are delivered regularly to reinforce concepts and maintain skills between workshops, helping teams sustain momentum long after the initial training day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  SafeSide CARE's content fidelity is maintained through the InPlace® model, where a nominated host from your organization convenes the group — no specialist facilitation training required. The content is embedded directly in the videos.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is fidelity supported over time? Is there follow-up or reinforcement?">
    In the InPlace® Learning model, teams work through interactive video modules together with a nominated host from your organization who convenes the group. The content is embedded in the videos, so the host does not require specialized knowledge or training, thereby maintaining content fidelity throughout implementation.

    Rather than relying on a one-off workshop, SafeSide embeds ongoing connection and interaction throughout your team's learning journey through:
    - A **Community of Practice** where you can access tools and resources and ask questions of SafeSide faculty and peers worldwide.
    - **Weekly microlearnings** delivered straight to your inbox.
    - Access to exclusive **SafeSide Live Learning sessions** with clinical and lived experience faculty.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can SafeSide CARE be tailored to the specific needs of my organization?">
    Absolutely. While SafeSide CARE offers a foundational approach to suicide prevention applicable across various settings, SafeSide also works closely with organizations to tailor the training content, ensuring it aligns with your team's unique challenges and context.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What kind of support can I expect after subscribing to SafeSide CARE?">
    Subscribers gain access to video-based training year-round, monthly Q&A sessions with experts, and a growing library of tools and refreshers. SafeSide's team also provides ongoing updates to ensure your practices remain at the forefront of suicide prevention research and guidelines.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does SafeSide include voices of lived experience?">
    Lived experience is at the heart of everything SafeSide does. The organization was founded on collaboration between clinical and lived expertise, guided by a core belief in "better together." Lived experience is embedded in all content — from co-designing modules to delivering live sessions and shaping ongoing learning.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
