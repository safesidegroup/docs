InPlace® Learning is SafeSide's workforce education model — and it is built around a simple insight: a one-off training event is rarely enough to change behavior at scale. Rather than deliver a single workshop and walk away, InPlace® Learning blends the consistency and repeatability of video-based teaching with the depth of group interaction, the expertise of live faculty sessions, and the accessibility of ongoing tools and refreshers. The result is a model your whole workforce can use, no matter where or when they work.

## Three Reinforcing Components

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Video-Guided InPlace Workshops" icon="circle-play">
    Specially designed video modules guide the workshop group through clinical content, real demonstrations, and structured discussion prompts — building a common framework and shared language across your team. No facilitation expertise is needed. Any staff member can host the session. It works in-person, hybrid, or fully remote, and can be repeated for new staff without scheduling an external trainer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SafeSide Live Learning Sessions" icon="chalkboard-user">
    SafeSide faculty — clinical specialists and people with lived experience — run two types of regular live sessions open to all members. **Monthly Questions & Updates** give staff a space to share experiences, ask questions, and connect with peers from other organizations. **In Focus** sessions are one-hour expert-led deep dives into complex or frequently raised topics — such as suicide risk in children and young people. Between live sessions, the online Community of Practice keeps the conversation going.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tools and Refreshers" icon="rotate">
    Short refresher modules and updated content are released throughout the year — ideal for onboarding new staff, revisiting skills that have drifted, or responding to changes in clinical guidelines. This is what makes InPlace® Learning sustainable over time: your investment compounds, and every new team member can reach the same standard as existing staff without waiting for the next scheduled training day.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The Host Role

One of the most practical features of InPlace® Learning is that it does not require a trained facilitator or subject matter expert to run. Any staff member can step into the host role. Here is what that involves:

- Convene the group — organize the time, space, and attendees for the session
- Advance the video — press play and manage the on-screen content as it guides the group
- Keep discussion on track — prompt the group with the provided discussion questions and help the conversation stay focused

The host does not need any specialist knowledge in suicide prevention. Everything needed to get started is contained in a two-page handout: **Getting Started with InPlace Learning** — available in your SafeSide account. A 10-minute overview video also walks through the full process so hosts feel confident before the session begins.

## Built for Distributed and Shift-Based Workforces

Many health and community organizations face a structural challenge: their workforce is never in one place at the same time. InPlace® Learning is designed specifically for this reality.

<Tip>
  Because sessions are video-guided and can be hosted by any staff member, you can run the same workshop across multiple sites, shifts, or teams — without an external facilitator traveling to each location or a small pool of internal trainers becoming a bottleneck.
</Tip>

Organizations that benefit most from InPlace® Learning include those with:

- Staff working across multiple sites or in remote locations
- Shift-based rosters that make coordinating a whole-team training difficult
- High staff turnover or regular onboarding needs
- A need for consistent training outcomes regardless of location or team

## More Than a One-Off Workshop

InPlace® Learning is not a training event — it is an ongoing subscription to a community of practice and a continuously updated content library. Here is why that matters:

The research is clear that behavior change requires more than a single exposure to new information. Suicide prevention skills need to be rehearsed, discussed, and reinforced over time — especially in high-stress environments where practice can drift between training events.

With InPlace® Learning, your workforce stays connected to current best practice through monthly live sessions with SafeSide faculty, regular refresher modules that respond to updated clinical guidance, and a community of peers across other organizations who are navigating the same challenges. New staff are onboarded to the same standard without needing to wait for a scheduled training day.

<Note>
  InPlace® Learning is built on more than 10 years of research. The model traces its origins to the Commitment to Living (CTL) program — the first brief training to demonstrate objectively rated improvement in assessment documentation — and has since been validated with mental health professionals, youth services workers, and primary care teams. [Explore the evidence base](https://safesideprevention.com.au/articles/an-overview-of-the-evidence-base-for-inplace-learning).
</Note>
