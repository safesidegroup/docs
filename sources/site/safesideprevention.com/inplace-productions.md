# Media and Marketing for Public Health Initiatives

InPlace Productions® a division of SafeSide Prevention, bridges the gap between creative agency capabilities and public health expertise. We help transform prevention initiatives into impactful, scalable programs.

InPlace Productions® collaborated with Groundswell Services to produce skill demonstration videos that demonstrate best practices for forensic evaluation report writing. These include teachings on how and what questions to ask, what information must not be included in the report, and how to assess rational understanding.
