# What is measured as part of SafeSide Prevention's standard evaluation?

When participants consistently complete the pre-and post-evaluation that is offered standard as part of your work with SafeSide Prevention, we can measure workshop completion and several important constructs, including self-efficacy and learning transfer. When rated positively, these particular constructs are demonstrated to increase our confidence that participants will integrate the skills and concepts in their day-to-day work with people at risk.
