# Can I receive continuing education credits for participating in a SafeSide InPlace Workshop?

Participants who are eligible for Continuing Medical Education (CME) credits can request **3 AMA (American Medical Association) Category 1 CME credits** when they submit their post-workshop evaluation. The CMEs are provided by the University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry and accredited by ACCME to certify continuing medical education for physicians. If you requested CMEs when you submitted your post-evaluation, the University of Rochester issues these. The requests are processed once a quarter and you will receive a notification from SafeSide with instructions on how to access your CME transcript as soon as the University makes them available. 

**If you plan to request credit from a different licensing or professional organization,** [**feel free to use the attached document**](https://safesideprevention.com/media/documents/SafeSide-InPlace-Workshop-Description-and-Learning-Objectives.pdf)**.**
