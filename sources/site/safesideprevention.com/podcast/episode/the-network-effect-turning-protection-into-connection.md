**Episode Description:**

In this bonus episode of _Never the Same_, host Professor Tony Pisani shares his keynote from the National Suicide Prevention Conference in Australia.

Tony explores how the structure of our relationships—not just how supported we feel—shapes suicide prevention, mental health, and how groups function. Using examples from high schools, the United States Air Force, first responders, community organisations, and work in Australia and New Zealand, he shows how shared connections and group cohesion can protect people who may never voice their distress.

This episode invites listeners to adopt a “network lens” and consider how stronger, more connected groups can support wellbeing and prevention.

**Host:** 

- **Professor Tony Pisani** is a professor, clinician, and founder of SafeSide Prevention, leading its mission to build safer, more connected military, health, education, and workplace communities.
