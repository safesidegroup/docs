# Never the Same

"Never the Same" is an interview-based podcast that explores the intersection of suicide prevention, career development, and life lessons. Inspired by Heraclitus' quote, "No man ever steps in the same river twice, for it's not the same river and he's not the same man," the podcast reflects on the ever-changing nature of life and personal growth.

Each episode will feature conversations with thought leaders from various fields, highlighting defining moments and transformative shifts in thinking. Through these dialogues, the podcast uncovers fresh insights for suicide prevention while offering broader perspectives on personal and professional evolution.

Whether you’re a professional seeking growth, a leader navigating complex challenges, or simply someone ready to embrace a new perspective, this podcast will leave you inspired to act and rethink what’s possible.

Nicole Doyley, author of What About the Children?, joins Never the Same to explore what it means to grow up biracial, parent multiracial children, and build identity across cultures. Nicole shares her story of growing up in Brooklyn in a Black and white family, being “not Black enough” and “not white enough,” and how acceptance and cultural pride changed her life. In this inaugural episode of Never the Same, Professor Tony Pisani sits down with Dr. David Jobes, creator of the Collaborative Assessment and Management of Suicidality (CAMS), to discuss this transformative approach.
