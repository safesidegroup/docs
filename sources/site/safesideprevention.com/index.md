## The SafeSide Difference

#### Lived Experience + Clinical Expertise

SafeSide Prevention’s educational programs are co-led by clinical and lived experience  instructors blending professional insight with personal understanding.

#### Innovative Learning Approaches

Safeside Programs use the latest science and tools in blended learning to provide convenient yet deep learning experiences, both online and in-person. Our experts are adept at finding the right programs for you, including working with you to adapt them for your context.

#### Measurable Impact

Underpinned in ongoing research, SafeSide prioritizes transparency and accountability by offering metrics and data tools to help you measure your progress and improve as you go.

#### Approach to Zero Suicide

The SafeSideFramework provides a common language for identifying, engaging, treating, and transitioning at-risk individuals. This approach fosters system-wide change, aiming to reduce suicides to zero.
