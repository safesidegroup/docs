# Contact SafeSide

Questions about our programs? Choose how you'd like to be in touch.

#### [Chat](https://safesideprevention.com/#chat)

If we're not available, we'll get back to you within one business day.

#### [Schedule a Call](https://safesideprevention.com/schedule-a-call)

Appointments for phone/video calls are usually available within a couple of days.

#### [Email Us](https://safesideprevention.com/contact-request)

We will respond to your message within one business day.

## Resources to contact if you need crisis help

SafeSide does not provide crisis intervention. But if you reached this page looking for help, these crisis resources are there for you:

**United States** [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://chat.988lifeline.org/) 
 
**Australia** [LifeLine Australia](https://www.lifeline.org.au/)

**Canada** [Crisis Services Canada](https://www.crisisservicescanada.ca/en/)

**New Zealand** [LifeLine](https://www.lifeline.org.nz/)
