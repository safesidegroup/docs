## Frequently Asked Questions

What is SafeSide Schools?

A program that combines student suicide prevention with staff wellness support. It strengthens team cohesion and helps schools keep their best staff.

What does working with SafeSide look like?

Services engage with SafeSide in different ways depending on their goals, workforce needs, and service setting. Some organizations begin with a single workshop or program, while others build longer-term partnerships focused on practice consistency, workforce support, implementation, or culture change across teams and systems.

Once you connect with us, you’ll be guided through a simple step-by-step process — starting with a call to understand your goals, followed by planning and building the right pathway for your context. You can read more about the full process on our [What to Expect page](https://safesideprevention.com/what-to-expect-with-safeside).

How is training delivered?

Core Connections is delivered in interactive group sessions. SafeSide CARE is delivered through InPlace® Learning with workshops and continuous learning opportunities. Both can be scheduled flexibly to fit around deployments and operational demands.

How is it different from other trainings?

Minimal prep. Built for real schools. Designed for all staff. Addresses both student safety and staff wellbeing.

How does SafeSide Schools fit with other SafeSide programs?

SafeSide Schools is part of the wider SafeSide system. Core Connections supports staff wellbeing and team culture, while CARE gives every adult a clear role in student safety. Together, they create one shared language and approach across classrooms, offices, and leadership.

What evidence supports this approach?

**CARE**: Built on 15+ years of suicide prevention research.

**Core Connections**: Proven in randomized controlled trials with military personnel, showing reduced depression, suicide risk, and occupational problems.

Will this replace our crisis protocols?

No. It strengthens them. Staff respond earlier and more effectively with evidence-based practices, clear boundaries, and documentation tools.

What about cost?

Priced per school building—often less than one day of substitute coverage.

Can our school train in-house facilitators?

Yes. In the early stages, SafeSide provides expert facilitators. Once your school or district decides to sustain the programme long-term, we offer a well-established pathway to prepare your own staff to deliver with fidelity and scale.

Does SafeSide provide facilitators?

CARE: Any staff member can host using video and AI support tools.

Core Connections: Expert facilitators lead sessions.

What is the time commitment?

Tiered approach that fits PD schedules:

\- Universal (All Staff): 30-min eCourse, Targeted (Teachers, Aides, Coaches, Admins): 2-hr workshop

\- Intensive (Mental Health Staff): 2-hr workshop

\- Core Connections (Staff Wellness): 4-hr training (in a half-day or two shorter sessions).
