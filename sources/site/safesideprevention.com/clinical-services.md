# Being prepared to respond with confidence and care

Health and human services need more than standalone training. They need practical approaches that strengthen teamwork, support consistent care, improve staff confidence, and help organizations respond well before, during, and after suicide-related crises. SafeSide supports services across the full continuum of suicide-specific care.

### Core Connections

An interactive program that strengthens team connections and resilience, reduces isolation, and supports staff well-being.

### SafeSide CARE

CARE gives clinicians a shared framework for person-centered suicide-specific care, helping organizations build greater consistency, confidence, and collaboration across teams and roles.

### CAMS Treatment

CAMS engages patients as partners in care and supports structured, suicide-specific treatment that helps clinicians address the drivers of suicidal distress with clarity and fidelity.

### SafeSide Restore

A network that helps services implement post-incident processes that promote healing, improvement, and a restorative learning culture.

## Frequently Asked Questions

What support does SafeSide provide for clinical services?

SafeSide brings together four connected solutions — Core Connections, SafeSide CARE, CAMS, and Restore — to support clinical services across workforce well-being, suicide-specific care, treatment, and restorative learning after critical incidents.

All programs come with implementation support and consultation, and can include leadership engagement, workforce development, and ongoing partnership, depending on the needs of the service.

What does working with SafeSide look like?

Services engage with SafeSide in different ways depending on their goals, workforce needs, and service setting. Some organizations begin with a single workshop or program, while others build longer-term partnerships focused on practice consistency, workforce support, implementation, or culture change across teams and systems.

Once you connect with us, you’ll be guided through a simple step-by-step process — starting with a call to understand your goals, followed by planning and building the right pathway for your context. You can read more about the full process on our [What to Expect page](https://safesideprevention.com/what-to-expect-with-safeside).

What is the time commitment?

Core Connections: 4–6 hours of interactive team sessions

SafeSide CARE: Half-day InPlace® Workshops with ongoing learning

CAMS: Structured training plus fidelity support

Restore: Bi-monthly network meetings

How is it delivered?

All programs are designed to fit busy clinical schedules:

\- Core Connections: interactive team sessions

\- CARE: InPlace® video-based workshops with continuous learning

\- CAMS: structured training with support

\- Restore: ongoing network meetings 

What is the cost?

Pricing varies by service size and delivery format. Most services find the cost is less than a single lost training day, with the return of stronger teams, more confident care, and consistent practice.

Can we start small and scale?

Yes. Many services begin with CARE and CAMS in priority teams, then add Core Connections for well-being and Restore to strengthen culture after incidents.
