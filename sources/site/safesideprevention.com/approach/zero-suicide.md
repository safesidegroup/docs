# Suicide Prevention in Systems

SafeSide Prevention delivers a systems-based approach to suicide prevention. Our programs cultivate a healthy, cohesive workforce, ready to respond and supported when suicide occurs. SafeSide Prevention Founder and University of Rochester Professor Tony Pisani provides an overview of Suicide Prevention in Systems (Pisani, Moutier, & Stahl, 2021; Pisani & Boudreaux, 2023), how this relates to Zero Suicide, and the programs SafeSide offers to advance this approach.

- Workforce Education
- Policy and practice review and recommendations

## SafeSide Serves...

SafeSide partners with organizations in the United States, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and the United Kingdom, including:

- University of Rochester Recovery Center of Excellence: SafeSide has collaborated with the UR Medicine Recovery Center of Excellence, with funding from the Health Resources & Services Administration, to deliver a no-cost [eLearning](http://www.safesideprevention.com/withyou) and podcast focused on the important role friends and family can play in the lives of individuals using substances and at risk for suicide. Through this same partnership, SafeSide has delivered the SafeSide Program to thousands of staff working with at-risk people in rural areas across the US.
- State of Utah Department of Health and Human Services: In collaboration with DHS, hundreds of staff supporting Utah youth and families have experienced a SafeSide Program. This includes hosting a full-day youth suicide prevention workshop annually and rolling out the SafeSide Program to all staff within Utah Juvenile Justice and Youth Services.
- Lexington Health: Developing from the AFSP partnership, Lexington Health began as a small pilot in a few practices supported by the South Carolina chapter of AFSP. This beginning has grown into a full health system implementation, reaching over 60 locations and over 1,000 doctors, nurses, other health care professionals and administrative supports.

# Core Values

### More than Safety

Safety is essential, but not the final goal. We'll help you meet patient safety goals and regulations, but we aim higher: toward recovery, health, and happiness.

### Healthy Team

We prioritize the health, prosperity, and connectedness of our team, so our partnership with you will be warm, productive, and uplifting.

### Better Together: Clinical and Lived Experience

SafeSide educational programs are co-led by a suicide prevention expert and instructors with diverse lived experience.

### Measure Your Impact

SafeSide training grew out of programs and studies conducted by Dr. Tony Pisani at the University of Rochester. Research on the approach is ongoing. We suggest metrics and provide data to assist our subscribers in measuring their impact and progress.

### Meliora

Meliora (“always better!”) is a core value of SafeSide Prevention. We offer subscriptions, ongoing contact, and continually updated materials to keep up with advances in the field and to respond to feedback and requests from our subscriber community.
