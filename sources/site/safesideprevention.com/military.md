# Every Unit Has a Mission. Every Service Member Has a Role.

SafeSide fosters unit trust and cohesion by providing commanders and supervisors with clear early-response skills. Leaders act decisively, mental health teams respond consistently, and service members know they are supported. Together, Connect and CARE form one system.

### Core Connections

An interactive, evidence-based program that strengthens unit connections, reduces isolation, corrective training, and suicide risk, and supports retention.

### SafeSide CARE

Practical tools for commanders, supervisors, and health teams. CARE provides a shared framework for responding to distress, reinforcing trust and operational readiness.

### Evidence and Evaluation

Built-in evaluation tools measure confidence, skills use, and cohesion. Results show lasting reductions in depression and suicide risk, stronger units, and measurable gains in readiness.

### Looks Like Your Force

SafeSide adapts content, language, and visuals so training reflects your unit’s identity and culture. From small enhancements to full integration, customization ensures programs fit seamlessly.

When it comes to safety planning, "what" you do matters, but "how" you do it matters more. SafeSide CARE is created through clinical and lived experience expertise and provides evidence-based strategies, such as developing safety plans for foreseeable changes, and engaging support networks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How does SafeSide work with the military?

SafeSide combines Core Connections (unit resilience and cohesion) with SafeSide CARE (leadership and crisis response skills). Together, they strengthen readiness, trust, and protection across the force.

What is the time commitment?

Core Connections: 6–8 hours across three sessions.

SafeSide CARE: 2–3 hour modules for supervisors, leaders, and health staff. Both are delivered flexibly to fit operational demands.

How is this different from other training?

Unlike traditional lectures, SafeSide uses interactive programs with facilitated experiential learning in Core Connections and InPlace® Learning in CARE. Both are efficient, evidence-based, and built to support without overloading personnel.

How do we get started?

We consult with your command to understand your needs and schedule. Together, we design an implementation plan that fits your priorities and operational tempo.

What about cost?

Pricing varies by unit size and delivery format. Most commands find that the cost is less than a single lost training day, with the return of stronger cohesion, lower attrition, and fewer corrective actions.

How does this address hidden risk?

Core Connections reduces isolation and strengthens cohesion. CARE equips leaders to step in early with a clear, consistent response. This dual approach protects those who might otherwise be missed.

How do we know it works?

A gold-standard RCT with 1,500 Airmen showed Core Connections reduced suicide risk, depression, and corrective training. CARE evaluations show confidence in responding rose from 72% to 95%.

How is training delivered?

Core Connections is delivered in interactive group sessions. SafeSide CARE is delivered through [InPlace® Learning](https://safesideprevention.com.au/articles/an-overview-of-the-evidence-base-for-inplace-learning) with workshops and continuous learning opportunities.

Does this replace existing protocols?

No. SafeSide complements current requirements by strengthening networks, improving responses, and ensuring consistency across the chain of command.

Can our units train in-house Core Connections facilitators?

Yes. Early on, SafeSide provides expert facilitators. Once the program is established, we prepare in-house trainers to deliver with fidelity and scale.

Can SafeSide be customized for our units?

Yes. SafeSide adapts content, language, and visuals so training reflects your unit’s identity and culture. Options range from minor enhancements to full integration with command priorities.
