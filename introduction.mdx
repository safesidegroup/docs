SafeSide Prevention is a suicide prevention education, consultation, and evaluation platform that helps government, healthcare, military, and community organizations define and achieve their prevention goals. Founded by University of Rochester Professor Tony Pisani and built on over a decade of research and real-world testing, SafeSide equips your workforce with the knowledge, skills, and shared language needed to identify, engage, and support people at risk — and to build the kind of organizational culture where that support can truly take root.

## The SafeSide Difference

SafeSide is built on four pillars that distinguish it from conventional training programs. Together, they ensure that what your team learns is not only evidence-based but immediately applicable, measurable, and sustained over time.

## Who SafeSide Serves

SafeSide partners with organizations across five countries — the **United States**, **Australia**, **Canada**, **New Zealand**, and the **United Kingdom** — spanning a wide range of sectors and settings. Whether you work in a hospital system, a government agency, a military branch, or a school district, SafeSide's programs are designed to meet you where you are and scale to your needs.

- **Healthcare** — hospitals, primary care clinics, behavioral health systems, and health networks
- **Government** — state and local departments of health and human services, juvenile justice, and youth services
- **Military and First Responders** — active duty personnel, veterans services, police, and workplace programs
- **Schools** — youth-serving organizations, educational institutions, and community programs

No matter your sector, SafeSide works alongside your leadership and workforce to embed prevention into everyday culture and practice — not as a one-off event, but as a lasting capability.
