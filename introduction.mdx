SafeSide Prevention is a suicide prevention education, consultation, and evaluation platform that helps government, healthcare, military, and community organizations define and achieve their prevention goals. Founded by University of Rochester Professor Tony Pisani and built on over a decade of research and application, SafeSide equips your workforce with the knowledge, skills, and shared language needed to identify, engage, and support people at risk.

## Who SafeSide Serves

SafeSide partners with organizations in the **United States**, **Australia**, **Canada**, **New Zealand**, and the **United Kingdom**, across a range of sectors and settings:

- **Healthcare** — hospitals, primary care clinics, behavioral health systems, and health networks
- **Government** — state and local departments of health and human services, juvenile justice, and youth services
- **Military and First Responders** — active duty personnel, veterans services, police, and workplace programs
- **Schools** — youth-serving organizations, educational institutions, and community programs

SafeSide works alongside your leadership and workforce to embed prevention into everyday culture and practice, not as a one-off event, but as a lasting capability.
