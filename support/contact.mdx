SafeSide offers three ways to connect — chat directly on the website, schedule a phone or video call, or send an email. Whichever channel you choose, the team is responsive and focused on helping you understand which programs are the right fit for your organization.

<Warning>
  **SafeSide does not provide crisis intervention.** If you or someone you know needs immediate support, please reach out to a crisis service in your region:

  - 🇺🇸 **United States:** [988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://chat.988lifeline.org/)
  - 🇦🇺 **Australia:** [LifeLine Australia](https://www.lifeline.org.au/)
  - 🇨🇦 **Canada:** [Crisis Services Canada](https://www.crisisservicescanada.ca/en/)
  - 🇳🇿 **New Zealand:** [LifeLine](https://www.lifeline.org.nz/)
</Warning>

## Ways to Reach SafeSide

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Chat" icon="message-circle">
    Start a live chat directly on the SafeSide website. If the team is not available when you reach out, they will follow up with you within one business day.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Schedule a Call" icon="phone" href="https://safesideprevention.com/contact">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Email" icon="mail" href="https://safesideprevention.com/contact-request">

  </Card>

## What to Have Ready

To help the SafeSide team give you the most useful response as quickly as possible, have the following information on hand when you reach out:

- **Organization name** — the name of your hospital, health system, school, company, or agency
- **Country** — SafeSide works with organizations across the US, Australia, and beyond, and regional context helps the team point you to the right resources
- **Programs of interest** — which SafeSide offerings you are curious about, such as InPlace® Learning, SafeSide Care, or clinical consultation
- **Workforce context** — any relevant details about your team, such as size, roles, existing training programs, or specific challenges you are trying to address

<Tip>
  You do not need to have everything figured out before reaching out. The SafeSide team regularly helps organizations think through which program configuration makes the most sense for their workforce and goals.
</Tip>
